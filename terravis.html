About Terravis Energy

Terravis Energy is the clean heating and cooling subsidiary of Worksport Ltd., commercialising AetherLux™, a cold-climate heat pump that keeps heating through frost instead of stopping to defrost.

Every conventional air-source heat pump has to pause heating and reverse into cooling mode to melt ice off its outdoor coil, as often as every hour in damp, near-freezing weather. ZeroFrost™ technology prevents the frost from forming in the first place, so AetherLux delivers continuous heat with no reverse-cycle defrost, no defrost meltwater and no backup electric heat in the test conditions published by the company.

Terravis has secured a U.S. patent on the technology, a volume manufacturing partner, a competitive NTAP award with the U.S. Department of Energy’s NREL to analyse ZeroFrost in Alaska, and a government evaluation for a potential long-term supply deal. Commercialisation is planned within 2026.

Boilerplate

Its AetherLux™ heat pump uses patented ZeroFrost™ technology to prevent frost from forming on the outdoor coil, delivering continuous heating in cold climates without the reverse-cycle defrost interruptions of conventional heat pumps. Using the Terravis and AetherLux brands

  • Company name: “Terravis Energy” (title-case, two words). Product: “AetherLux”. Technology: “ZeroFrost”. Use the ™ on first reference.
  • Terravis yellow is the only accent on the corporate mark. ZeroFrost blue is reserved for ZeroFrost technology callouts.
  • Keep clear space around the wordmark equal to the height of the “T”. Do not outline, recolour, rotate or add effects.
  • On first reference describe Terravis as “a subsidiary of Worksport Ltd. (Nasdaq: WKSP)”.
