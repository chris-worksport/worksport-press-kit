Company

About Worksport

Worksport is a U.S. manufacturer of hard tonneau covers, solar integrations and portable power for the light truck, overlanding and consumer markets, built at its 152,000 sq. ft. campus in West Seneca, New York.

Founded in 2011 as a producer of pickup truck bed covers, Worksport now designs, develops and manufactures its flagship NEXUS and HD3 covers in-house and owns the intellectual property behind them. Its SOLIS solar tonneau and COR portable battery generator unite as a mobile energy system, and its subsidiary Terravis Energy is commercialising the ZeroFrost™ cold-climate heat pump.

Worksport products are sold direct at worksport.com, on Amazon, through a growing dealer and distributor network across North America, and, from late 2026, through the Slate Auto Marketplace.

Boilerplate

Worksport Ltd. (Nasdaq: WKSP) is a U.S.-based manufacturing enterprise that, through its subsidiaries, designs, develops, manufactures, and owns the intellectual property on a variety of tonneau covers, solar integrations, portable power systems, and clean heating & cooling solutions. Additionally, Worksport’s hard-folding cover, designed and manufactured in-house, is compatible with all major truck models and is gaining traction with newer truck makers including the electric vehicle (EV) sector. Worksport seeks to capitalize on the growing shift of consumer mindsets towards clean energy integrations with its proprietary solar solutions, mobile energy storage systems (ESS), and Cold-Climate Heat Pump (CCHP) technology. Terravis Energy’s website is terravisenergy.com.

Usage guidelines

Brand guide (PDF)

Using the Worksport brand

  • On light backgrounds the emblem is blue and the word-mark black; on dark backgrounds use the white word-mark with the blue emblem, or the all-white logo. One-colour use is black on light or white on dark only.
  • Keep clear space on every side equal to the width of the “P” in the word-mark. The emblem may stand alone in blue, black or white; never outlined.
  • Do not add effects, rotate, stretch or place the logo on busy backgrounds without the white version.
  • Write the company name as “Worksport”. Use “Worksport Ltd.” and “(Nasdaq: WKSP)” on first reference in financial coverage.
  • Product names are capitalised: NEXUS, HD3, AL4, AL3, SC4, SC3, SOLIS, COR. Terravis Energy, AetherLux™ and ZeroFrost™ are title-case.
  • Photography in this kit is cleared for editorial use about Worksport. Credit “Photo: Worksport Ltd.” where credits are used. Not for advertising or resale.

Steven Rossi
Founder & Chief Executive Officer
Headshot (JPG) LinkedIn

Steven Rossi founded Worksport in 2011 and has led the company from a tonneau cover start-up to a Nasdaq-listed American manufacturer with its own 152,000 sq. ft. factory in Western New York. Under his leadership Worksport brought production of its covers to the United States, launched the SOLIS solar tonneau and COR portable energy system, and in 2026 introduced NEXUS, the fastest-growing product in the company’s history.

He is available for interviews on U.S. manufacturing, the truck accessory aftermarket, mobile and off-grid power, and the company’s growth. Requests go through the media contact below.
