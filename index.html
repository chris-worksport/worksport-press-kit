About Worksport

Worksport is a U.S.-based manufacturing enterprise that designs, builds and owns the intellectual property behind hard tonneau covers, truck-bed solar, portable power and, through its subsidiary Terravis Energy, cold-climate heat pump technology. Covers are made at the Worksport Campus in West Seneca, New York, with research and development in Ozark, Missouri.

Founded in 2011 as a producer of pickup truck bed covers, Worksport now manufactures its flagship NEXUS and HD3 covers in-house at its 152,000 sq. ft. campus. SOLIS, a solar tonneau cover, and COR, a portable energy system, bring clean power to the truck bed, and Terravis Energy is commercialising AetherLux™, a Cold-Climate Heat Pump (CCHP) with patented ZeroFrost™ technology that heats continuously without defrost cycles.

The enterprise sells direct at worksport.com and on Amazon, through a dealer and distributor network across North America that now includes The AAM Group, Meyer Distribution and Tri-State.

Boilerplate

Worksport Ltd. (Nasdaq: WKSP) is a U.S.-based manufacturing enterprise that, through its subsidiaries, designs, develops, manufactures, and owns the intellectual property on a variety of tonneau covers, solar integrations, portable power systems, and clean heating & cooling solutions. Additionally, Worksport’s hard-folding cover, designed and manufactured in-house, is compatible with all major truck models and is gaining traction with newer truck makers including the electric vehicle (EV) sector. Worksport seeks to capitalize on the growing shift of consumer mindsets towards clean energy integrations with its proprietary solar solutions, mobile energy storage systems (ESS), and Cold-Climate Heat Pump (CCHP) technology. Terravis Energy’s website is terravisenergy.com.

    Brand guide (PDF)

    Using the Worksport brand

    • On light backgrounds the emblem is blue and the word-mark black; on dark backgrounds use the white word-mark with the blue emblem, or the all-white logo. One-colour use is black on light or white on dark only.
    • Keep clear space on every side equal to the width of the “P” in the word-mark. The emblem may stand alone in blue, black or white; never outlined.
    • Do not add effects, rotate, stretch or place the logo on busy backgrounds without the white version.
    • Write the company name as “Worksport”. Use “Worksport Ltd.” and “(Nasdaq: WKSP)” on first reference in financial coverage.
    • Product names are capitalised: NEXUS, HD3, AL4, AL3, SC4, SC3, SOLIS, COR. “Active Lock Technology” (NEXUS) is title-case with its own symbol. Terravis Energy, AetherLux™ and ZeroFrost™ are title-case.
    • Photography in this kit is cleared for editorial use about Worksport. Credit “Photo: Worksport Ltd.” where credits are used. Not for advertising or resale.

    Steven Rossi
    Founder & Chief Executive Officer
    Headshot (JPG) LinkedIn

    Steven Rossi founded Worksport in 2011 and has led it from a tonneau cover start-up to a Nasdaq-listed manufacturing enterprise with its own 152,000 sq. ft. campus in Western New York. Under his leadership Worksport brought production of its covers to the United States, launched the SOLIS solar tonneau and COR portable energy system, formed Terravis Energy to commercialise the AetherLux™ cold-climate heat pump, and in 2026 introduced NEXUS, the fastest-growing product in the company’s history.
